# Upload your recorded narration here

Preferred filename: **hackistan-voiceover.wav**

Upload one continuous, dry voice recording: WAV preferred (48 kHz, 24-bit if available; 44.1 kHz/16-bit also fine). MP3 or M4A is acceptable if that is what your recorder exports—keep its real extension.

No background music, sound effects, heavy noise removal, compression or loudness normalization. Leave a little room at the beginning/end. Speak naturally; do not rush or stretch the recording to exactly 60 seconds. Retakes can be separate files with clear names.

After upload, tell the agent the filename. It will inspect the recording, synchronize captions, adjust scene timing where needed, and balance Foley beneath the voice before adding any music. Nothing activates automatically merely because a file is uploaded.

This is a public GitHub repository: uploads committed here will be public. To avoid publishing your raw voice recording, attach it directly in the Codex chat instead.
