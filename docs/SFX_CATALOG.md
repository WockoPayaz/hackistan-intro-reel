# Foley asset and license catalog

14 unique CC0 sources: six openly published Freesound HQ MP3 previews, six original WAV files from OwlishMedia’s recorded household pack, and two lossless FLAC pencil recordings. No synthetic SFX, Mixkit files or Pixabay files are included.

**Material-identification limitation:** OwlishMedia’s stationery files are numbered. The pack describes real paper, scissors, masking tape and stapler recordings, but does not identify each object per file. Adhesive/sticker/CTA assignments below describe their use in this mix; they are not verified literal recordings of sticker adhesive or a rubber stamp. Pencil erasing is used as a quiet drawing texture.

MP3 previews remain MP3; they were not relabeled as lossless originals. Only selected archive members are retained; the larger source bundles and unrelated recordings are not committed.
